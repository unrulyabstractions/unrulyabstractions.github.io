The behavior direction is the difference of W and N mean activations over reply tokens, learned leave-one-out: trained on every other WeirdChat prompt, tested on the held-out pair. Chance is 0.50. The old token-set directions on the same activations stay at target ≈ decoy.

Pilot — letter-probe identification

One prompt per case (highest behavior incidence), arms W/N. A concept from the four-entry menu is injected into the introspector at read time; it answers a five-way letter question (four concepts + "none of these"), averaged over 8 label permutations. The injected concept's column is outlined; the row argmax is green. The none rows are the zero-injection confabulation floor.

Pilot — free-text answers, judged

The same trials, asked open-endedly and sampled 8 times; claude-haiku-4-5 classifies each answer against the menu. With nothing injected the model says "none"; with a concept injected at strength 2 its answer floods with the concept's vocabulary instead of naming it — enactment rather than report.
